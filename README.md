# Angry Andy - Model Leisure Centre

The follow-up to *Angry Andy Gets The Gig*. Andy has a new gig: **Duty Manager at the Model Leisure Centre**. He is still permanently annoyed. Get through the day (07:00 to 21:00, about 5 real minutes) without his anger hitting 100%.

A small top-down pixel-art browser game, built on the same engine, art style and controls as the first game.

## The leisure centre

Swimming pool, gym, office, staff kitchen, toilets, changing rooms, cafe, reception and a bowling alley, with the car park out front.

Sharon runs reception, Bev runs the cafe and Gaz is (supposedly) watching the pool. Treadmill Tina and Lunchtime Len are on the treadmills, Gains Gary is by the weights, League Barry and Brian are bowling and Carol and Jan are at their coffee morning.

## How it plays

Random **tasks** pop up in the panel on the right, each with a timer. Walk to the marker and **hold the action key** until the bar fills. Miss the timer and Andy's anger goes up; finish it and he calms down a little and scores points.

- **Customer at reception**: check their membership card, then **Let in** or **Turn away**. The photo must be theirs, the plan must cover what they want to do (swim, gym or bowling) and the date must not have passed. A wrong call costs anger.
- **Troublemakers**: Kyle, Tyler and Jayden dive-bombing the aqua aerobics or running round the pool, a chip fight in the cafe, Stag-do Steve bowling down the wrong lane, and Gains Gary dropping weights. Having a go at them calms Andy the most.
- **Cleaning**: puddles by the pool, sick in the bowling alley, crisp packets in the cafe. Mop it up.
- **Alistair's back**: Alistair is banned, but he sneaks in for a free coffee from the cafe machine. Catch him and throw him out before he gets away with it.
- **Lane pins jammed**: reset the bowling pinsetter.
- **Treadmill broken**: ERROR 404. Fix it.
- **Lifeguard asleep**: Gaz has nodded off on his high chair. Wake him up before somebody drowns.
- **Vending machine**: it has eaten Little Alfie's pound. Thump it (very calming).
- **Complaint at reception**: Mrs Pemberton (usually). **Fob off** calms Andy; **Free swim** voucher is safe but winds him up a little.
- **Phone calls**: the office phone or Andy's mobile. Fob off, sort it out, or hang up on cold callers.
- **Area Manager visit**: every third fob-off in a day (or at random) the Area Manager wants a meeting in the office. Andy is stuck there for a few seconds while the other timers keep running.
- **Lycra Lee** cycles in and hogs the changing room shower. He isn't even a member.
- **Loo alert**: Gains Gary has had three protein shakes and is heading for the toilets. Stop him, or they're out of order (for Andy too).
- **Bladder** and **thirst** rise slowly. Use the **toilets** and make **tea** in the **staff kitchen**.
- The day starts gently and gets busier. Each day (Monday to Sunday, round again) is harder, and half of Andy's anger carries over.

**Controls:** WASD or arrow keys to move. Hold E or Space to act. A/D (or 1/2) for the two buttons on a card check or call. P pauses, M mutes. On a phone or tablet, an on-screen joystick and ACT button appear when you touch the screen.

## Run it

```bash
npm install
```

```bash
npm run dev
```

Open the printed URL. `npm run build` makes a static site in `dist/`.

```bash
npm test
```

```bash
npm run smoke
```

`npm test` runs the unit tests, including headless simulations of full days. `npm run smoke` drives the real page in headless Chromium and saves screenshots to `smoke-output/` (it needs Playwright).

## Share it

```bash
npm run package
```

This makes **one file**, `release/AngryAndyLeisureCentre.html`, with the game, the pictures and the fonts inside it. People double-click it to play in their browser. High scores are saved in each person's own browser.

## Changing things

Everything you'd want to tweak is in `src/data/`:

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `catchphrases.json` | Andy's catchphrases and grumbles, troublemakers' excuses and shouts, the Area Manager's lines, Alistair's lines, complaints and the vending machine lines. |
| `phonecalls.json` | Who rings, what they want, Andy's fob-offs, plus the cold callers and their pitches. |
| `tuning.json` | Opening hours, day length, walking speeds, bladder and thirst, difficulty ramp. |
| `events.json` | Each task type: how often it appears, time limit, hold time, anger penalty, calm reward, points. |
| `characters.json` | The cast, where staff and regulars stand, and the looks used to draw the placeholder art. |
| `map.json` | The floor plan (`#` wall, `.` floor, `~` pool, `L` bowling lanes), rooms, furniture and named spots. |

Art: see [ASSETS.md](ASSETS.md). All pictures are generated by `tools/make-placeholder-art.mjs` (`npm run art`).

## Code layout

- `src/main.ts`: boot and the game loop.
- `src/game/`: rules, as plain TypeScript with no DOM. `game.ts` (tasks, needs, scoring), `spawner.ts` (random tasks and difficulty), `membership.ts` (the card check), `meters.ts`, `catchphrases.ts`.
- `src/world/map.ts`: the tile map, collision and A* pathfinding.
- `src/entities/person.ts`: Andy and everyone else.
- `src/render/renderer.ts`: draws the centre, the HUD, the task panel and the pop-ups on one canvas.
- `src/core/`: assets loader, keyboard and touch input, synthesised sound effects.
